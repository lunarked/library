    Demand forecast Reorder and safety stock Inventory health and aging Peak season

    Tinted cells are yours to fill in. Every other figure is calculated from them. The forecast feeds the reorder planner, and both feed the peak season deadlines.

    Adjusted demand scales each month's sales up to a full month in stock, so a stockout does not drag the forecast down. The forecast averages your recent months with seasonality removed, then applies each month's seasonal index and your growth rate.

    Safety stock = z × √(lead time × daily demand variability² + daily demand² × lead time variability²), with z taken from your service level. Low stock flags anything under 28 days of cover, where Amazon's low-inventory-level fee starts. Peak dates and cutoffs in the sample are examples: check Seller Central for each year's real dates.
