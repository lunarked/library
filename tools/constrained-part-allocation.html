Why the rules give different answers

The golden screwA cheap part can stop an expensive vehicle. The body, motors and seats are already bought and installed, so a vehicle missing one part is cash sitting in a parking lot.

By marginBuild the vehicles that earn the most first. It makes the most money this quarter, but cheap lines get nothing, so a launch or a contract can fail.

By backlogSplit the parts in proportion to the orders each line has waiting. No customer waits much longer than another, but every line runs short.

Launch firstGive the new model everything it needs, then fill the rest by margin. The launch stays on time, but the older lines pay for it.

Must-haves, then marginCover the hard limits first (the launch minimum and the fleet contract), then fill by margin. Most planners end up here, but the line with the most waiting orders gets cut.
