Why each part gets a different answer

Late reorder means lost salesChip wafers take 18 weeks. If you lock too few and the device is a hit, new wafers arrive after the launch window is over. Those sales are gone.

Expensive and hard to resellDisplays are the most costly part and a custom size has almost no resale value. Locking extra costs a lot if it flops, and a reorder still arrives early enough to save most hit sales.

Short reorder timeMemory and enclosures arrive a few weeks after a reorder. If demand is a hit you can catch up early in the launch window, so locking the forecast is enough.

Late parts can waitAssembly slots and boxes lock after pre-orders open. By then you know roughly how many to order, so they never need a guess.

Earlier pre-orders helpEvery week that pre-orders open sooner lets one more part wait for real demand. Move the slider and watch the gap between plans shrink.
