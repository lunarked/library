    How the model works

    • Inflow: fleet × weekly damage rate × seasonality (summer peak), plus rework coming back from last week's repairs.
    • Parts: repairs whose part is out of stock (1 − fill rate) sit in "awaiting parts" for the lead time, then join the repair queue.
    • Capacity: techs × shifts × days × hours × (1 − absenteeism) × UPH. Overtime is only worked when the queue needs it. New hires run at 50% UPH for their first 3 weeks.
    • Under the roof (UTR): every scooter in the building, queued or waiting on parts, is off the street. Availability = 1 − UTR ÷ fleet; turnaround uses Little's law (UTR ÷ daily output).
    • Money: paid labor (OT at 1.5×) + parts + site overhead. Lost ride revenue = scooter-days off the street × revenue per scooter-day.
